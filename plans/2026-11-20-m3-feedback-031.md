## Implementation and coverage

1. **Session persistence and minimum duration.** Reproduce repeated checkpoint
   failure with a real Room database and sub-millisecond start timestamps; fix
   identity comparison at database precision. Keep retries and log actual failures
   without blaming storage. Default minimum session to 30 seconds, editable from
   1–300 seconds in Tracking. Apply consistently to qualification, recovery,
   summaries and prompts. Changing the setting must not remove accepted history.
2. **Book assessment, history and completion.** Replace favorite switch with an
   accessible filled/outline heart with a brief pop in both directions. Align
   half-star fill with the outline. Use a clear state selector, compact labelled
   rating/favorite grouping, an integrated review action, and history rows with
   dates and summaries together; secondary history management goes in a menu.
   Use Android/Material date pickers for start/end/finish dates, retaining date
   validation and explicit deletion confirmation. Hold the full celebration after
   its introduction until tap or its eased-in close button; preserve quiet mode,
   restoration and skip behavior.
3. **Reader, settings and shared controls.** Status line has page left, tracking
   state centered, clock/battery right with a charging bolt. Center notice content
   vertically. Give all off switches distinct thumbs/tracks in light, dark and
   reader themes. Add vertical breathing room to settings titles. Add persistent
   System/Light/Dark app appearance, independent of reader theme, plus About with
   real version/license/source/privacy information.

## Design

Retain Deft's paper/ink palettes, Space Grotesk headings/body, Space Mono dates
and counters, purple actions and green completion. Use existing 16dp screen
gutters, 16dp card padding, 8–12dp within groups and 20–24dp between sections.
Reading state and heart are direct controls; rating is one aligned group. History
shows a chronological structure rather than three equally prominent text actions.
Adapt at large text without clipping or hiding functionality. Motion respects
system animation scale; targets remain at least 48dp.
