01 · the mark

three candidates, pick one

Original shapes in kmab's line grammar: one stroke weight, square caps, mitred joins. Each detaches one stroke, the way kmab's λ does.

Two pages, split at the spine. The gap is the detail.

Standing up. The ribbon runs past the bottom edge.

One continuous spread with a detached ribbon. Current placeholder.

CHAPTER 7 · THE MESSAGE

The rain had stopped an hour before dawn, and by the time she reached the archive the street was dry enough to show the old chalk marks again. Someone had counted something on the wall, five strokes at a time, and then given up.

She read them the way she read everything now: slowly, then all at once. Forty-three. A number with no unit, waiting for one.

Inside, the clerk did not look up. He turned a page, and then another, and she understood that he was not reading at all, only keeping time.

"You're early," he said. It was not a complaint.
